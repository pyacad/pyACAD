States

Every control has four states and every surface has three. Most unfinished-looking software is missing the unglamorous ones: disabled, busy, and empty. A control that changes only on hover is not in a state, it is hiding.

Control states

rest · pressed (darken the fill) · focused (2px accent ring, offset 1) · disabled

disabled: surface fill, muted text, muted border, no hover response, no hand cursor

A disabled control always says why, in a caption beside or beneath it: "3 names would collide", "Select what to move first". Disabled with no explanation is a dead end. Never fake disabled with opacity: the control stays clickable and the text falls below contrast.

Surface states

No shortcuts yetBundles that declare a chord appear here.

empty: what would be here, and what puts something here

busy: quiet placeholders at the shape of the content, no spinner theatre

Preview did not loadCheck the log for details.

error: plain sentence, no apology, and the action that depended on it goes disabled

Rules

1. Disabled is a real visual state, not reduced opacity, and it carries a reason.
2. Pressed exists for every button weight; a control that does not move under the pointer feels dead.
3. Focus is always visible, on every focusable thing, including list rows and custom controls.
4. Every list, panel, and preview declares its empty state and its failure state before it ships.
5. Busy states appear for anything that can take more than about a second, and pair with progress and cancel when the work is a batch.
